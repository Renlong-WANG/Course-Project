# Current figure captions

All five figure captions use normal weight throughout.

## Figure 1

Training design and independent residual calibration. Blue squares indicate training inputs selected for space coverage and active refinement near the limit-state boundary. Green circles indicate iid calibration inputs from $\mathbb P$, independent of $\mathcal D^{\mathrm{tr}}$, with $\hat g$ fixed. Dashed curves are input-density contours; shading marks $g(\bm x)\le0$. The geometry and point counts are illustrative.

## Figure 2

Pointwise KL and DW acceptance regions. Blue denotes KL acceptance; gold adds probabilities accepted only by DW. The inset magnifies the left tail. Boundaries are computed numerically using the illustrative parameters shown; $q$ is displayed continuously between empirical levels $j/N^{\mathrm{te}}$.

## Figure 3

Simultaneous bounds on the response CDF. Blue shading shows the interval between the lower and upper bounds on $p_f(c)$; the gold segment at $c=0$ recovers the original failure probability interval.

## Figure 4

Partitions of the residual support and input space. For $K=3$, three singletons and four open intervals partition $\Xi$. Half-open intervals of $-\hat g(\bm x)$ induce the input cells, with masses $w_i=\mathbb P(\mathcal X_i)$.

## Figure 5

Joint partition of the input and residual spaces. For $K=3$, each cell has mass $\pi_{ij}=\Pi(\mathcal X_i\times\Xi_j)$. Blue, gold, and red denote safe, overlap, and failure cells, respectively.
