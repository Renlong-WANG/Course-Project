3. Ambiguity Set Design

   Failure occurs when ξ ≤ −ĝ(X), so the residual cumulative distribution function (CDF) gives the
marginal mass at or below each failure threshold. We use the Dümbgen–Wellner (DW) construction
(Dümbgen and Wellner, 2023) to control empirical CDF deviations simultaneously over all thresholds,
with tolerances that vary across probability levels.
   Let q and p denote an empirical probability and a candidate probability. Following Berk and Jones
(1979) and the CDF conﬁdence bands of Owen (1995), we measure their local discrepancy by the Bernoulli
Kullback–Leibler divergence

                                          q               1−q
                       ϕ(q, p) := q log     + (1 − q) log     ,          ∀q ∈ [0, 1], p ∈ (0, 1),              (4)
                                          p               1−p

where logarithms are natural and a term with zero numerator is interpreted as zero. For q close to an
interior p, the approximation ϕ(q, p) ≈ (q − p)2 /[2p(1 − p)] scales the deviation by the Bernoulli variance.
Thus, the same absolute deviation receives a larger weight near the probability boundaries. The full
divergence also covers zero and full empirical counts through ϕ(0, p) = − log(1 − p) and ϕ(1, p) = − log p.

5. Implementation Procedure

   The proposed method serves as a post-training extension of existing surrogate modeling workﬂows
and applies to any measurable surrogate predictor, including Gaussian process predictors, polynomial
chaos expansions, and neural networks. Once ﬁtting and model selection are complete, an existing
independent test set can be used to calibrate residual uncertainty without additional evaluations of g.
For a ﬁxed surrogate and input law, the same calibration serves the entire response-threshold curve,
while the closed-form bounds require neither an explicit joint partition nor the solution of an LP.

      The computational complexity of the proposed method is assessed in terms of its additional cost
relative to an existing surrogate modeling and reliability analysis workﬂow. For ﬁxed ϕ and ψ, the DW
calibration thresholds can be tabulated oﬄine by (N te , δ) using the ﬁnite-sample recursion of Dümbgen
and Wellner (2023, Supplement, Section S.7), and the scalar acceptance endpoints can be precomputed
by (N te , θ). Given the existing test responses and surrogate predictions, residual computation, sorting,
and counting require O(N te log N te ) operations, followed by O(K) table lookups to construct the rank
bounds. Once the required surrogate-response CDF values are available, the closed-form bounds can be
evaluated in O(K) time per response threshold. Consequently, excluding oﬄine precomputation and CDF
evaluation, the overall incremental computational complexity for L response thresholds is O(N te log N te +
LK). Although the ﬁnite-dimensional reformulation involves O(K 2 ) joint cell-mass variables, the closed-
form expressions eliminate the need to explicitly construct these variables or solve any optimization
problem. The near-linear calibration complexity and linear-time evaluation per response threshold thus
make the proposed method an eﬃcient and scalable extension of existing surrogate-based reliability
analysis workﬂows.
      To preserve the statistical guarantees, the test data must remain independent of surrogate ﬁtting
and model selection, and ĝ must remain ﬁxed during and after calibration, as speciﬁed in Section 2.
Conservative numerical evaluation uses an upward approximation of θ and outward approximations of
the rank bounds, rounding lk downward and uk upward. When a veriﬁed residual support is available,
the endpoint adjustments in Remark 1 can be incorporated directly.

    With exact input event probabilities and θ ≥ θN te (δ), Corollary 1 guarantees simultaneous coverage
of at least 1 − δ, conditional on Dtr . When the surrogate-response CDF is estimated by independent
auxiliary simulation, conservative bounds can be obtained by substituting a simultaneous CDF conﬁ-
dence band’s upper and lower limits into (32) and (33), respectively. If the band has conditional error
probability at most δ aux given both datasets, the resulting simultaneous coverage is at least 1 − δ − δ aux .
Any statistical error arising from simulation-based DW threshold calibration must also be accounted for
in the overall conﬁdence budget.
