3. Ambiguity Set Design

    Failure occurs when ξ ≤ −ĝ(X), so the residual cumulative distribution function (CDF) gives the
marginal mass at or below each failure threshold. Accurate tail control is needed for small failure prob-
abilities. We use the Dümbgen–Wellner (DW) construction (Dümbgen and Wellner, 2023) to control
empirical CDF deviations simultaneously over all thresholds, with tolerances that vary across probability
levels.
    Let q and p denote an empirical probability and a candidate probability. Following Berk and Jones
(1979) and the CDF confidence bands of Owen (1995), we measure their local discrepancy by the Bernoulli
Kullback–Leibler divergence

                                          q               1−q
                       ϕ(q, p) := q log     + (1 − q) log     ,          ∀q ∈ [0, 1], p ∈ (0, 1),                (4)
                                          p               1−p

where logarithms are natural and a term with zero numerator is interpreted as zero. For q close to an
interior p, the approximation ϕ(q, p) ≈ (q − p)2 /[2p(1 − p)] scales the deviation by the Bernoulli variance.
                                                            2
Thus, the same absolute deviation receives a larger weight near the probability boundaries. The full
divergence also covers zero and full empirical counts through ϕ(0, p) = − log(1 − p) and ϕ(1, p) = − log p.
At the boundaries of the candidate probability, we set ϕ(0, 0) = ϕ(1, 1) = 0, ϕ(q, 0) = +∞ for q > 0, and
ϕ(q, 1) = +∞ for q < 1.

The threshold θ controls the corrected likelihood discrepancy; the resulting CDF tolerances vary with the
probability level and can be asymmetric about the empirical CDF. For fixed test data, 0 ≤ θ1 ≤ θ2 implies
QDW (θ1 ) ⊆ QDW (θ2 ). The lower failure bound is therefore nonincreasing in θ, and the upper bound is
nondecreasing. Increasing the prescribed confidence level gives a nondecreasing calibrated threshold and
hence a wider or unchanged interval.

dence on the input. Because the joint ambiguity set includes all couplings with the specified marginals, a
gap can remain even when the residual marginal is known exactly. The same coverage argument applies
to any residual ambiguity set containing Q⋆ with conditional probability at least 1 − δ given Dtr .
